Finance Tracker

Welcome to the Finance Management System. Here you can add, view, and manage your financial records easily. Use the menu to navigate, click "Add" to create new records, and check summaries to track your totals. Everything is organized to help you manage your finances quickly and efficiently.

How to Use This Website

  • Use the navigation bar to move between pages.
  • Click buttons to add or manage data.
  • Fill in all required fields before submitting forms.
  • Check summaries to see totals and reports.
💡 Tip: Always double-check your data before submitting to avoid errors.
